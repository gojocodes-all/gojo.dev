Providence Heights

A modern school website that turns a wide set of academic, community and admissions information into a confident, easy-to-navigate experience.

  • TypeSchool website
  • StackHTML, CSS, JavaScript

The challenge

A school website serves several audiences at once: prospective families, current students, parents, staff and the wider community. Each group looks for different information, but the experience still needs to feel like one school.

The project required a clear structure for the school story, academics, facilities, leadership, student life, gallery, admissions and enquiries—without making the homepage feel like a list of disconnected sections.

The approach

I shaped the page around trust and discovery. The first sections establish the school’s identity and values, then visitors move naturally into academics, learning spaces, leadership and student life before reaching admissions and contact.

Large imagery, strong headings and repeating card patterns give the site a modern school identity while keeping long-form information easy to scan on mobile and desktop.

  • Junior and senior secondary academic sections
  • CBT centre and practical science facilities
  • Leadership and prefect-body profiles
  • Student life, activities and gallery sections
  • Admissions call to action and enquiry form
  • Responsive navigation and scroll animations

The build

The site is deliberately framework-free. Semantic HTML provides a clear content outline, CSS handles the responsive layout and visual system, and lightweight JavaScript powers the mobile navigation and reveal interactions.

This keeps the deployment straightforward while making the code easy to update as the school adds staff photography, activities and admissions information.

  • HTML5
  • CSS3
  • JavaScript
  • Responsive design
  • Semantic structure

The outcome

The finished website gives Providence Heights a stronger digital presence and a central place to explain what the school offers. Families can move from the school’s identity to concrete academic and facility information, then continue into an admissions enquiry.

The modular structure also gives the project room to grow without requiring a full redesign whenever new school content becomes available.
