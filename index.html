About GOJO.DEV

Learning fast. Building for real.

“Good design should look intentional, feel simple and help someone take the next step.”

I’m Owojuyigbe Oluwajomiloju, a web developer and digital designer based in Lagos. GOJO.DEV is where I turn curiosity into useful products—from school websites and educational platforms to e-commerce experiences and messaging tools.

In 8+ months of focused learning, I have moved beyond tutorials into projects with real audiences, content, constraints and goals. Each build is a chance to improve the code, sharpen the design and make the experience clearer.

I currently work across HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Node.js, Express and MongoDB, while continuing to strengthen accessibility, performance and full-stack development.
